Purpose of the COE plans

  • Provide a structured process for planning, implementing, and evaluating college initiatives.
  • Ensure compliance with COE accreditation requirements.
  • Guide continuous improvement of academic programs, student services, and operational practices.
  • Support evidence-based decision making through assessment and evaluation.
  • Align institutional goals with workforce and community needs.

Requirements

All 10 COE plans must:

  • Be developed, implemented, and reviewed annually.
  • Include input from faculty, staff, students, and external stakeholders where appropriate.
  • Document measurable goals, outcomes, and evaluation results.
  • Be available for review during the accreditation cycle and reaffirmation visits.
